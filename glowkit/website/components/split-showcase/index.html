Split Showcase

Two partner cards joined into one capsule by a dotted seam; the hovered half springs outward and rounds its inner corners.

Category Cards & BordersCrate glowkit-interactionsFeature interactionsegui 0.37
Try it liveMore Cards & Borders

Usage

Add glowkit next to eframe:

cargo add glowkit eframe

Then draw it inside any egui Ui:

use glowkit::prelude::*;
use glowkit::interactions::{SplitShowcase, ShowcaseSide};

ui.add(SplitShowcase::new(
    ShowcaseSide::new("Northwind", "HOSTING PARTNER"),
    ShowcaseSide::new("Lumen", "ANALYTICS PARTNER").mark(1, None),
).shift(50.0));

Prefer to own the code? The glowkit CLI copies this component's source into your project, shadcn-style:

cargo install glowkit-cli
glowkit add split-showcase

About Split Showcase

Split Showcase is one of 38 components in glowkit's Cards & Borders category. It lives in the glowkit-interactions crate, enabled by the interactions feature of glowkit (on by default). It reads its colors from the active glowkit theme, so it matches the rest of your app in light, dark or a custom brand theme, and every color, size and timing has a setter to override it.

Cards with personality: border beams and shine borders, spotlight and magic cards that follow the pointer, tilt and glare, glass surfaces, pixel and decay reveals, and bento grids. Content inside stays fully interactive.
