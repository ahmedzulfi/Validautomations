18 components · Navigation

Animated navigation components for egui

Menus and navigation that move with intent: bubble, pill, gooey and staggered menus, card navs, floating and resizable navbars, sidebars, command palettes, tables of contents and morphing tab bars.

Open in the live galleryAll categories

All Navigation components

Bubble Menu

Navigation

A round menu button that bursts into tilted pill links on bouncy springs; hovered pills straighten and flood with color.

Card Nav

Navigation

A rounded navbar whose hamburger expands it downward into a row of colored link cards that slide up one by one.

Pill Nav

Navigation

A capsule of pill links: hovering raises a circle from below that floods the pill while its label rolls over.

Staggered Menu

Navigation

A full-height side menu: colored layers sweep in, staggered, followed by a panel of huge numbered links rising into place.

Infinite Menu

Navigation

A sphere of round tiles you drag to spin; it coasts, snaps the nearest tile to the front and shows its title and action.

Sidebar

Navigation

A collapsible app sidebar with brand header, sections, badges, a sliding active indicator and a user footer; collapses on a spring into an icon rail with tooltips.

Floating Navbar

Navigation

A floating pill navbar that slides away while you scroll down and comes back as soon as you scroll up.

Resizable Navbar

Navigation

A full-width header that shrinks into a narrower floating, rounded bar with border and shadow once the page scrolls.

Command Palette

Navigation

A ⌘K / Ctrl+K palette: modal search with grouped fuzzy results, a gliding highlight and full keyboard navigation.

Animated Link

Navigation

Text links with animated hover treatments: an underline that draws in and leaves the other way, a highlighter sweep, and an underlined link with a turning arrow.

Table of Contents

Navigation

An “On this page” list with a rail whose accent indicator follows the section you're reading (scroll-spy); click to jump.

Section Index

Navigation

Contacts with sticky headers and an A-Z bar: scrub it and the letters swell like a dock while a bubble pops out beside the finger.

Action Bar Sheet

Navigation

Command menus that collapse into a ⋯ drawer on phones: rows slide sideways into each menu's items, and the drawer drags and flicks away.

Trail Nav

Navigation

A navigation stack with breadcrumbs and a back link: pages slide with parallax, swipe from the edge to pop interactively, hold Back for the history.

Glass Tabs

Navigation

A tab switch whose selection is a thick liquid-glass lens that magnifies the track, squeezes in flight and wobbles as it lands.

Morph Navbar

Navigation

A dark pill nav that springs open into its own dropdown, one shell resizing between a picture reel, an accordion and a link list.

Shared Layout Background

Navigation

One soft background slides and resizes between list rows or nav links under the pointer, instead of each item lighting up on its own.

Bounce Sidebar

Navigation

Sidebar links with a dot that hops to the one you pick: it leaps out in an arc, stretches in flight and lands with a squash.
