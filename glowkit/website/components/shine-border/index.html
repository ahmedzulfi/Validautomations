Shine Border

A band of multi-colored light drifts diagonally around the card's border.

Category Cards & BordersCrate glowkit-effectsFeature effectsegui 0.37
Try it liveMore Cards & Borders

Usage

Add glowkit next to eframe:

cargo add glowkit eframe

Then draw it inside any egui Ui:

use glowkit::prelude::*;
use glowkit::effects::ShineBorder;

ShineBorder::new().colors(&palettes::MAGIC).show(ui, |ui| { ui.heading("Shine"); });

Prefer to own the code? The glowkit CLI copies this component's source into your project, shadcn-style:

cargo install glowkit-cli
glowkit add shine-border

If you know this pattern from Magic UI, Shine Border is the Rust version: an original egui implementation, not a port, that runs natively and in WebAssembly.

About Shine Border

Shine Border is one of 38 components in glowkit's Cards & Borders category. It lives in the glowkit-effects crate, enabled by the effects feature of glowkit (on by default). It reads its colors from the active glowkit theme, so it matches the rest of your app in light, dark or a custom brand theme, and every color, size and timing has a setter to override it.

Cards with personality: border beams and shine borders, spotlight and magic cards that follow the pointer, tilt and glare, glass surfaces, pixel and decay reveals, and bento grids. Content inside stays fully interactive.
