Direction Aware Hover

A card whose caption overlay slides in from the edge the pointer entered through, and leaves the way it exits.

Category Cards & BordersCrate glowkit-blocksFeature blocksegui 0.37
Try it liveMore Cards & Borders

Usage

Add glowkit next to eframe:

cargo add glowkit eframe

Then draw it inside any egui Ui:

use glowkit::prelude::*;
use glowkit::blocks::DirectionAwareHover;

ui.add(DirectionAwareHover::new(|p, r| paint_photo(p, r))
    .title("In the mountains")
    .subtitle("$1299 / night"));

Prefer to own the code? The glowkit CLI copies this component's source into your project, shadcn-style:

cargo install glowkit-cli
glowkit add direction-aware-hover

About Direction Aware Hover

Direction Aware Hover is one of 38 components in glowkit's Cards & Borders category. It lives in the glowkit-blocks crate, enabled by the blocks feature of glowkit (on by default). It reads its colors from the active glowkit theme, so it matches the rest of your app in light, dark or a custom brand theme, and every color, size and timing has a setter to override it.

Cards with personality: border beams and shine borders, spotlight and magic cards that follow the pointer, tilt and glare, glass surfaces, pixel and decay reveals, and bento grids. Content inside stays fully interactive.
