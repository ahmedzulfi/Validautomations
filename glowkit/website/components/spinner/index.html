Spinners

Eighteen loader styles in one widget: rings, arcs, dots, bars, pulses, orbits, waves, grids, helixes and more.

Category Loaders & ProgressCrate glowkit-componentsFeature componentsegui 0.37
Try it liveMore Loaders & Progress

Usage

Add glowkit next to eframe:

cargo add glowkit eframe

Then draw it inside any egui Ui:

use glowkit::prelude::*;
use glowkit::components::{Spinner, SpinnerKind};

ui.add(Spinner::new(SpinnerKind::Orbit).size(32.0));
ui.add(Spinner::new(SpinnerKind::Gradient).size(32.0).speed(1.5));
ui.add(Spinner::new(SpinnerKind::Bars).color(hex("#22C55E")));

Prefer to own the code? The glowkit CLI copies this component's source into your project, shadcn-style:

cargo install glowkit-cli
glowkit add spinner

About Spinners

Spinners is one of 3 components in glowkit's Loaders & Progress category. It lives in the glowkit-components crate, enabled by the components feature of glowkit (on by default). It reads its colors from the active glowkit theme, so it matches the rest of your app in light, dark or a custom brand theme, and every color, size and timing has a setter to override it.

Spinners, progress rings and multi-step loaders that animate smoothly and stop repainting when they are done.
