34 components · 3D

Real-time 3D components for egui, no GPU setup

A software 3D renderer that draws lit, depth-sorted meshes straight into egui: model viewers with orbit controls, globes, particle clouds that morph between shapes, terrains, voxel text, 3D charts and carousels. No wgpu boilerplate, and it runs in WebAssembly too.

All 3D components

Flying Posters

3D

Posters ride a curved 3D drum: they swell as they pass the middle and tilt away over the top. Scroll or drag to fly.

3D Viewport

3D

The software 3D engine: meshes, materials (smooth, flat, toon, matcap, normal, wireframe, glow), lights, shadows, grid and 3D text, depth-sorted into egui meshes. Drag to orbit.

Model Viewer

3D

A lit 3D model floating over a soft shadow. Drag to orbit with inertia, pinch or ctrl+scroll to zoom; switch models, materials and the wireframe overlay.

Particle Morph

3D

Thousands of glowing points morph between a sphere, torus, wave, cube, helix and galaxy with a staggered, eased transition. The pointer pushes particles away; drag to orbit.

Wave Terrain

3D

An endless noise landscape the camera flies over: synthwave neon grid with a striped sun, a glowing wireframe, or flat-shaded low-poly hills.

Tilt Cubes

3D

A grid of 3D cubes that tilt to face the pointer; click to send a ripple through the grid. Wanders and ripples on its own when idle.

3D Card Carousel

3D

A ring of gradient cards spinning in 3D with perspective text and a glossy floor reflection. Drag sideways to spin it with inertia.

3D Flip Card

3D

Cards that flip over in 3D with a springy overshoot when clicked and tilt towards the pointer while hovered.

Globe 3D

3D

A true-3D lit globe: continents as thousands of dots, glowing arcs with comets between cities, pulsing markers, a graticule and an atmosphere halo. Drag to spin.

Galaxy

3D

A spiral galaxy of thousands of twinkling stars with differential rotation, nebula dust and a glowing core; the camera swoops through it on a cinematic path.

Warp Starfield

3D

Fly forward through a true-3D field of streaking stars. Hover to steer, press and hold to jump to warp speed.

Floating Shapes Hero

3D

Glossy gradient primitives float and spin in front of and behind a 3D headline, with pointer parallax: a Spline-style hero section.

Glow Knot

3D

A torus knot as glowing neon: a bright core line wrapped in layered bloom, light pulses racing along it and a faint wireframe tube. Drag to orbit.

Voxel Text

3D

Text built from glossy 3D voxel cubes (a tiny pixel font) rippling in a wave, swaying, and periodically bursting apart and snapping back together.

3D Bar Chart

3D

Data columns in a 3D grid that grow in with a staggered spring. Hover a bar to read it, drag to orbit, double-click to replay.

DNA Helix

3D

A rotating double helix of glossy spheres joined by colored base-pair rungs, fading into depth. Drag to orbit.

Puzzle Cube

3D

A 3x3x3 twisty puzzle cube that scrambles itself with smooth eased face turns. Drag to orbit.

3D Marquee

3D

A tilted perspective wall of cards whose columns scroll in alternating directions and fade out towards the edges.

Solar System

3D

A glowing sun with planets lit from the sun (real day and night sides) on orbit rings, a ringed giant and an orbiting moon. Drag to orbit, hover planets for names.

Prism Beam

3D

A glass prism that refracts a white beam into a rainbow fan with real Snell's law; drag to aim the light and watch the spectrum sweep.

Crumple Carousel

3D

Posters in a row where only the focused one lies flat; neighbours crinkle and the outer ones are balled up, smoothing out as they reach the middle.

Pleated Strip

3D

An endless column of posters joined by paper pleats and rolled round a drum; scrolling fast throws the folds open, at rest they close and the nearest poster centres.

DNA Carousel

3D

Pictures wound onto two opposing helix strands that drift past, the far ones blurred and colour-fringed like a lens edge.

Arc Carousel

3D

Cards riding a very large circle, the focused one upright at the apex and the rest tilted tangent to the curve.

Wave Gallery

3D

An endless ribbon of glossy cards bent into a wave in depth, titles printed into the surface, far cards sinking into fog.

Image Tunnel

3D

An endless 3D corridor with pictures on all four walls and a fine wireframe gliding toward you; click to pause, hold to rush.

Spiral Slider

3D

Curved picture panels wound onto a helix that screws along its own track, the far side frosted through the gaps.

Particle Globe

3D

Continents drawn as thousands of points on a slowly rotating sphere; drag to spin it and it keeps its momentum.

Particle Saturn

3D

A point-cloud ringed planet: a Fibonacci-sphere body inside wide particle rings with dark divisions; inner rings orbit faster. Drag to spin with inertia.

Cosmic Orb

3D

A glass sphere holding a turning galaxy: drifting nebula, twinkling stars, a lensed rim with chromatic fringes. It leans toward the pointer.

Single Glass Keyboard

3D

A single glass keycap refracting orange, magenta and blue studio light into rainbow fringes; it floats, tilts after the pointer and presses on click.

Cobalt Sprite

3D

A blue sprite with pill eyes inside a thick glass capsule, clear above and frosted below; it watches the pointer, bobs and blinks.

Corolla Gleam

3D

A crown of thick iridescent glass slabs standing in a ring: it turns, breathes and leans toward the pointer while thin-film colors slide across the glass.

Cylinder Carousel

3D

Round artworks wrapped around a drum: drag to roll it and they swing round, swelling, narrowing and dimming with the angle before snapping into place.
