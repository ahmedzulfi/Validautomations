Flying Posters

Posters ride a curved 3D drum: they swell as they pass the middle and tilt away over the top. Scroll or drag to fly.

Category 3DCrate glowkit-effectsFeature effectsegui 0.37
Try it liveMore 3D

Usage

Add glowkit next to eframe:

cargo add glowkit eframe

Then draw it inside any egui Ui:

use glowkit::prelude::*;
use glowkit::effects::FlyingPosters;

ui.add(FlyingPosters::new().textures(&my_textures).columns(3).speed(40.0));

Prefer to own the code? The glowkit CLI copies this component's source into your project, shadcn-style:

cargo install glowkit-cli
glowkit add flying-posters

About Flying Posters

Flying Posters is one of 34 components in glowkit's 3D category. It lives in the glowkit-effects crate, enabled by the effects feature of glowkit (on by default). It reads its colors from the active glowkit theme, so it matches the rest of your app in light, dark or a custom brand theme, and every color, size and timing has a setter to override it.

A software 3D renderer that draws lit, depth-sorted meshes straight into egui: model viewers with orbit controls, globes, particle clouds that morph between shapes, terrains, voxel text, 3D charts and carousels. No wgpu boilerplate, and it runs in WebAssembly too.

Tilt Cubes

A grid of 3D cubes that tilt to face the pointer; click to send a ripple through the grid. Wanders and ripples on its own when idle.

Particle Morph

3D

Thousands of glowing points morph between a sphere, torus, wave, cube, helix and galaxy with a staggered, eased transition. The pointer pushes particles away; drag to orbit.
