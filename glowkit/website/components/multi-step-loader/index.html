Multi Step Loader

An overlay listing the steps of a long task; each checks off in turn while the current one is highlighted and centered.

Category Loaders & ProgressCrate glowkit-blocksFeature blocksegui 0.37
Try it liveMore Loaders & Progress

Usage

Add glowkit next to eframe:

cargo add glowkit eframe

Then draw it inside any egui Ui:

use glowkit::prelude::*;
use glowkit::blocks::MultiStepLoader;

ui.add(MultiStepLoader::new(&[
    "Fetching the crate index", "Resolving dependencies", "Compiling shaders",
    "Baking gradients", "Polishing the glow", "Ready to ship",
]).step_duration(1.4));

Prefer to own the code? The glowkit CLI copies this component's source into your project, shadcn-style:

cargo install glowkit-cli
glowkit add multi-step-loader

About Multi Step Loader

Multi Step Loader is one of 3 components in glowkit's Loaders & Progress category. It lives in the glowkit-blocks crate, enabled by the blocks feature of glowkit (on by default). It reads its colors from the active glowkit theme, so it matches the rest of your app in light, dark or a custom brand theme, and every color, size and timing has a setter to override it.

Spinners, progress rings and multi-step loaders that animate smoothly and stop repainting when they are done.
