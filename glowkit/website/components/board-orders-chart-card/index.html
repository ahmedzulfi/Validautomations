Orders Chart Card

Monthly orders as this-year bars beside last-year bars; the headline counts up with a delta chip and hovering a month swaps in that month's figures.

Category ChartsCrate glowkit-interactionsFeature interactionsegui 0.37
Try it liveMore Charts

Usage

Add glowkit next to eframe:

cargo add glowkit eframe

Then draw it inside any egui Ui:

use glowkit::prelude::*;
use glowkit::interactions::BoardOrders;

let this = [1700.0, 1800.0, 2000.0, 1900.0, 2100.0, 2200.0, 2400.0, 2300.0, 2400.0, 2500.0, 2400.0, 2450.0];
let last = [1500.0, 1500.0, 1700.0, 1650.0, 1750.0, 1800.0, 1850.0, 1900.0, 1950.0, 2000.0, 1850.0, 1850.0];
ui.add(BoardOrders::new("Orders").data(this, last));

Prefer to own the code? The glowkit CLI copies this component's source into your project, shadcn-style:

cargo install glowkit-cli
glowkit add board-orders-chart-card

About Orders Chart Card

Orders Chart Card is one of 29 components in glowkit's Charts category. It lives in the glowkit-interactions crate, enabled by the interactions feature of glowkit (on by default). It reads its colors from the active glowkit theme, so it matches the rest of your app in light, dark or a custom brand theme, and every color, size and timing has a setter to override it.

Charts that draw in and respond to the pointer: line, area, bar, pie and donut, radar, radial, gauges, sparklines, scatter, heatmaps and calendar heatmaps, funnels, treemaps, candlesticks, Sankey diagrams and KPI tiles.

Earnings Chart Card

Charts

Stage Bars Card

Charts

A funnel as a list of rounded pills with value and share of the top stage, plus stat tiles; widths grow in and hovering a stage deepens it and fades the rest.

Most Active Days Card

Charts

A scrolling month calendar of mini activity rings with a month switcher; the selected day drives a full rings card beside it.

Contributions Card

Charts

A year of activity as a 53-week heat grid under a headline and stat tiles; tabs re-aggregate with a diagonal colour ripple and cells show their day on hover.

Steps Card

Charts

Seven teal pill bars under a count-up step total; the date pill pages through weeks and the hovered day gets an outline while the headline follows.

Activity Rings Card

Charts
