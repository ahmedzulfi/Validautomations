Gauge

Semicircular gauges with a gradient arc, tick marks and a needle that springs to its value.

Category ChartsCrate glowkit-chartsFeature chartsegui 0.37
Try it liveMore Charts

Usage

Add glowkit next to eframe:

cargo add glowkit eframe

Then draw it inside any egui Ui:

use glowkit::prelude::*;
use glowkit::charts::Gauge;

ui.add(Gauge::new(72.0).size(300.0).thickness(20.0).label("CPU load").unit("%"));
ui.add(Gauge::new(3.4).range(0.0, 5.0).needle(false).colors(&[cyan, violet]));

Prefer to own the code? The glowkit CLI copies this component's source into your project, shadcn-style:

cargo install glowkit-cli
glowkit add gauge

About Gauge

Gauge is one of 29 components in glowkit's Charts category. It lives in the glowkit-charts crate, enabled by the charts feature of glowkit (on by default). It reads its colors from the active glowkit theme, so it matches the rest of your app in light, dark or a custom brand theme, and every color, size and timing has a setter to override it.

Charts that draw in and respond to the pointer: line, area, bar, pie and donut, radar, radial, gauges, sparklines, scatter, heatmaps and calendar heatmaps, funnels, treemaps, candlesticks, Sankey diagrams and KPI tiles.
